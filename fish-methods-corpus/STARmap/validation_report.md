# Validation Report

Audit only. Independent review of the STARmap corpus; production files were not modified during this review.

## Review Scope

- Reviewed files / revision: `STARmap/index.md`, `STARmap/metadata.yaml`, `STARmap/description.md`, `STARmap/protocols.md`, `STARmap/probes.md`, `STARmap/benchmarks.md`, `STARmap/sources.md`, `STARmap/research_manifest.md` (audit only), as committed on 2026-10-15.
- Reviewer / independence limitations: The reviewer independently retrieved and inspected S01 (Wang 2018 *Science* primary publication, PMC6339868) and S03 (MicroHub / NIHMS-2117497 STARmap PLUS / RIBOmap / TEMPOmap joint protocol) full text via WebFetch, and confirmed the existence, public visibility, and star/fork counts of all five GitHub repositories cited (S04–S08). D01/D02 (Science supplementary workbooks) and the D03 RawImage directory inventory were not directly retrieved during this review. D04 (SCP1375), D05 (Zenodo 7332091) and S11 (Zeng 2023 *Nat Neurosci*) were confirmed at the accession level but their content was not fully extracted here. The review therefore relies primarily on independent S01/S03 retrieval plus cross-comparison against the Research Agent's existing transcription.
- Review date: 2026-10-15
