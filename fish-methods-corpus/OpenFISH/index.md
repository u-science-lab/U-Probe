# OpenFISH

## Official Name

OpenFISH (reported; S01, title and abstract).

## Full Name

No explicit acronym expansion established in the examined sources (S01 abstract; S03–S04 READMEs).

## Aliases

No additional official method alias established. MALDI–OpenFISH describes the integrated workflow, not a replacement name.

## Method Category

Imaging-based spatial transcriptomics; same-section spatial multi-omics (reported; S01 abstract).

## Primary Application

Spatial RNA measurement and integration with MALDI-MSI in tissue (S01 abstract; V01).

## Original Publication

Li X. et al. *OpenFISH enables same-section spatial transcriptomics and MALDI-MSI integration*. Neuron, online 29 September 2026. DOI: [10.1016/j.neuron.2026.09.007](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2026.09.007); PMID: 42810347 (S01). A 2025 preprint is recorded separately as S02.

## Brief Summary

Modular probe design and fluorescence imaging support spatial RNA analysis; the integrated workflow also registers MALDI-MSI data to cells. Research draft with material source-access gaps; not independently reviewed.

## Known Versions

V01: original 2026 publication. The 2025 preprint and later repository snapshots are tracked as source revisions; meaningful experimental version differences remain unresolved.
