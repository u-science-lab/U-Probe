# MERFISH

## Official Name

MERFISH. Reported in S01, Abstract.

## Full Name

Multiplexed error-robust fluorescence in situ hybridization (S01, Abstract; reported).

## Aliases

MERFISH. No distinct official short alias established in the inspected sources.

## Method Category

Multiplexed single-molecule RNA fluorescence in situ hybridization; spatial transcriptomics (S01, Abstract and Fig. 1; reported).

## Primary Application

Targeted measurement of RNA copy number and spatial location in individual fixed cells; subsequent developer implementations extend to tissue (S01, Abstract; S05, Fig. 4; reported).

## Original Publication

Chen KH, Boettiger AN, Moffitt JR, Wang S, Zhuang X. *Spatially resolved, highly multiplexed RNA profiling in single cells*. Science 348:aaa6090 (2015). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa6090); PMID 25858977. [S01](sources.md#s01).

## Brief Summary

Encoding probes tile target RNAs and collectively present readout sequences corresponding to a binary codeword. Sequential fluorescent readout hybridization and imaging measure the codeword; error-robust codes support identification and, for appropriate codes, correction (S01, Fig. 1 and Results; reported).
