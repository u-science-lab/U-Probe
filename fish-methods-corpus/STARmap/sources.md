## Secondary Sources

None identified for this draft. STARmap is documented by primary and official sources (S01–S11): the Wang 2018 Science paper, its supplementary materials, the STARmap PLUS / RIBOmap / TEMPOmap joint protocol (S03), the five software repositories (S04–S08), and the Zeng 2023 STARmap PLUS application paper (S11) with its Single-Cell Portal and Zenodo data deposits (S09, S10).
