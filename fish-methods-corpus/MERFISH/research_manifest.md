## Evidence Capture and Access Limits

Full official HTML/text snapshots are retained under evidence/ for S01/S03/S04/S05/S08/S09/S10, plus repository and resource-page records. Valid bDNA XLSX and DOCX files and the V04 supplemental PDF were downloaded. The V04 PDF was not fully examined. Binary endpoints for original S02 and V02 S13 returned HTML challenges/403/404; Europe PMC returned non-package XML for those two articles. These failures are not evidence that supplements do not exist. Failed payloads are renamed *.failed-response.* to avoid masquerading as spreadsheets or PDFs. Author example ZIP redirected to an S3 URL that returned 403. No archive content claim is based solely on its link. Search results were discovery aids; detailed production claims use downloaded primary/official text.

S12 was parsed with openpyxl; DOCX caption was read from document.xml. bDNA_sequence_checks.json records derived checks for all 16 bits. No published sequence letters were changed; whitespace removal is only a documented derived-analysis step. The repository was pinned to eba1735a4c350dbff31c06be1ab2df9d153e5c3d. Code was read but not run. Source byte hashes are in evidence/file_manifest.json.
