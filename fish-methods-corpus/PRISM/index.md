# PRISM

## Official Name

PRISM. [reported; S01, Abstract; V01/V02]

## Full Name

profiling of RNA in situ through single-round imaging. The author code styles the expansion as “Profiling of RNA In-situ through Single-round iMaging”. [reported; S01, Abstract; S06, README; V01/V02]

## Aliases

PRISM; no additional verified short method alias recorded. This entry concerns RNA PRISM, not the unrelated protein method Probe-based Imaging for Sequential Multiplexing. [S01, Abstract; S10, Abstract]

## Method Category

Multiplex RNA fluorescence in situ hybridization with padlock ligation, rolling circle amplification, and color-intensity readout. [reported; S01, Fig. 1a–f; V01/V02]

## Primary Application

Targeted spatial RNA imaging in mouse brain/embryo and human hepatocellular carcinoma. [reported; S01, Abstract; V01/V02]

## Original Publication

Chang T. et al. High-plex spatial RNA imaging in one round with conventional microscopes using color-intensity barcodes. Nature Biotechnology (published 30 October 2025). DOI: [10.1038/s41587-025-02883-7](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41587-025-02883-7); PMID: 41168495. Earlier author preprints exist [S05].

## Brief Summary

RNA-targeted padlocks produce localized RCA products. Competitive binding of labeled and unlabeled imaging probes produces multichannel intensity ratios used to identify RNA in one imaging round. [reported; S01, Fig. 1b–f; V01/V02]

## Known Versions

V01: 30/31-plex configuration. V02: 64-plex configuration, documented in the same publication; these are corpus configuration IDs, not official release names. Preprint revisions are tracked separately from assay configurations. [S01, Fig. 1e and Extended Data Fig. 7; S02]
