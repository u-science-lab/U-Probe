## Research-Stage Questions (Historical; Superseded)

1. The exact gene list of the 42-gene mouse visual cortex panel (Fig. 4 of S01) is in the Alon 2021 supplementary tables S9, S10 but the supplementary table content was not directly retrieved for this draft.
2. The exact gene list and probe sequences of the 34-gene mouse hippocampus panel (Fig. 5 of S01) are in the Alon 2021 supplementary tables S12, S13 but were not directly retrieved.
3. The exact gene list and probe sequences of the 297-gene HTAPP human metastatic breast cancer panel (Fig. 6 of S01) are referenced as using SOLiD chemistry but are not separately published as a probe table.
4. The exact Tm and GC parameter settings used by the Alon 2021 authors for the 42-gene visual cortex and 297-gene HTAPP panels are not given in the paper main text.
5. The exact BLAST database versions (RefSeq release dates) used by the Alon 2021 authors for the 42-gene visual cortex and 297-gene HTAPP panels are not given in the paper main text.
6. The complete SOLiD chemistry sequencing protocol (reagent kits, ligation mix compositions, imaging parameters for SOLiD dyes) is not documented in a dedicated protocols.io module; only the SOLiD barcode construction (logics, dibase matrix, primer offsets, ligation positions) is documented inside the Probe Generation protocol.
7. The 74-nt total padlock length does not exactly match the simple 5′→3′ sum of components reported in the assembly script (7+16+22+16+7 = 68 nt without the AAA spacers, or 80 nt with three AAA spacers); the published 74-nt figure corresponds to the `2*n+10` design criterion (`n = 32` homology length), which is consistent with the formula but does not match the simple component sum. The discrepancy is documented but not fully resolved in this draft.
8. The exact mRNA detection yield of the SOLiD chemistry for the HTAPP panel is not reported separately from the untargeted comparison numbers in S01 main text.
9. The exact SOLiD logics→nucleotides mapping for the 42-gene visual cortex panel and the 297-gene HTAPP panel is not directly retrieved.
10. The `prioritizedBarcodeLibrary.csv` file in the TargetedExSeqProbeGen repository is in logical (digits 0–3) format; the assembly step converts to nucleotides at probe-design time. The exact nucleotide sequences of all 326 barcodes are not directly inspected for this draft.
11. The ExSeqProcessing repository's last commit date and active maintenance status is not captured for this draft.
12. The exact reagent catalog numbers for the 297-gene HTAPP breast cancer panel padlock probes are not reported in the paper; they exist only in the authors' internal records.
13. The exact passivation chemistry reaction times and temperatures (beyond 30 min EDC/NHS at RT and 1 h ethanolamine-HCl at RT) are not detailed in the supplementary protocol; the paper main text describes the conversion of carboxylate to amide but does not give exact conditions.
