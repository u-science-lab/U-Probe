# Oligo-LiveFISH

## Official Name

Oligo-LiveFISH. Reported: S01, Introduction; V01.

## Full Name

Oligo pool-based LiveFISH. Reported: S01, Introduction; V01.

## Aliases

No additional interchangeable aliases established. LiveFISH is the earlier approach explicitly distinguished in S01; Oligopaint is not established as an alias or a version of this method. See the audit manifest for the routing discrepancy.

## Method Category

CRISPR-based live-cell genomic DNA imaging; fluorescent ribonucleoprotein (fRNP) pool labeling. Reported: S01, Results, design subsection; V01.

## Primary Application

Tracking repetitive and non-repetitive chromatin loci and enhancer–promoter dynamics in living human cells. Reported: S01, Summary; V01.

## Original Publication

Zhu Y. et al. (2025). High-resolution dynamic imaging of chromatin DNA communication using Oligo-LiveFISH. *Cell* 188, 3310–3328.e27. [DOI: 10.1016/j.cell.2025.03.032](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.03.032); PMID 40239646. S01.

## Brief Summary

Computationally selected guide pools are transcribed, fluorescently labeled, assembled with dCas9, and electroporated into cells for direct optical tracking. Reported: S01, Results, design subsection; V01.

## Known Versions

V01: original 2025 Oligo-LiveFISH; dual-RNA and sgRNA implementations and super-localization (SL-Oligo-LiveFISH) imaging are scoped implementations within the paper, not separate chronological releases. S01, Results and Table S1 (S02).
