## Supplementary Materials

Supplementary Data 1 (XLSX) contains all probe sequences used in the study, including:
- TDDN component sequences (T0a-d, T1a-d, T2a-d)
- Primary probe sequences (35 nt target + 30 nt readout)
- Fluorophore-labeled detection oligo sequences
- smFISH and HCR-FISH probe sequences for comparison

Supplementary Figures 1-9 provide additional validation and optimization data.

Available via the Nature Communications article supplementary materials (S01).
