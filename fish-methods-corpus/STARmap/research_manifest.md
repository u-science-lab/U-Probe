## Unresolved Research Questions

1. **STARmap probe component lengths in the original Wang 2018 paper**: The 19–25 nt target region, 6 nt complementary arms, and 12 nt reading region lengths are documented in the STARmap PLUS protocol (S03) but are they also in any part of the original Wang 2018 paper (S01) or its fig. S1? A direct fetch of fig. S1 and the supplementary materials PDF was not completed for this draft; the S03 protocol is treated as the design document of record for the 5'→3' component architecture.

2. **SEDAL cycle step-by-step parameters**: The room-temperature ligation, 60% formamide stripping, and 4-color imaging are confirmed; the exact number of SEDAL cycles per barcode (6 for 160/1020-gene; 7 for linear thick-tissue) is reported; but the per-cycle reagent concentrations (e.g., T4 ligase, reading-probe concentration) and the imaging exposure parameters are not directly extracted from S01 or S03 in this draft.

3. **Barcode-to-gene codebook**: The 5-nt barcode is on the padlock probe (REPORTED in S01), the barcode library size is 1,024 (4^5) (REPORTED in S01), and the actual barcode-to-gene assignment for the 160-gene panel (Table S1) and 1020-gene panel (Table S2) is not directly retrieved for this draft. The supplementary workbooks exist (S02) but their content was not extracted.

4. **Data availability and reproducibility for Wang 2018**: The Wang 2018 paper data availability statement directs readers to http://clarityresourcecenter.org and the supplementary materials. No GEO/SRA accession is reported in the paper. The only public raw-image data deposit traceable for Wang 2018 STARmap is the ClusterMap RawImage directory (S08). A full data-availability assessment requires direct download of the supplementary tables and a check of whether the paper has any later errata or corrigenda.

5. **STARmap version boundaries**: STARmap (V01, Wang 2018) and STARmap PLUS (V02, Zeng 2023 / Wang 2018-derived, with MA-NHS chemistry and protein co-detection) are recorded as separate version records in `metadata.yaml`. RIBOmap and TEMPOmap are sibling methods on the same SNAIL-STARmap platform (different probe designs: RIBOmap uses three probes; TEMPOmap uses click-chemistry labeling of U analogs) and are NOT treated as STARmap versions here. A future Research Agent investigating RIBOmap or TEMPOmap would create a separate method record.

6. **STARmap protocol and "STARmap PLUS" naming**: The joint MicroHub / NIHMS protocol (S03) covers STARmap, STARmap PLUS, RIBOmap, and TEMPOmap simultaneously. Whether STARmap (V01) and STARmap PLUS (V02) are best treated as one method (STARmap) with a versioned chemistry change, or as two distinct method names, is a judgment call. This draft records them as V01 and V02 of STARmap, with V02 distinguished by the MA-NHS chemistry and protein co-detection.

7. **STARmap probe-design software status**: The bavalab/snail-probe-designer (S05) is the canonical probe-design tool documented in the S03 protocol. Its maintenance status as of 2026 is not directly verified; the weallen/STARmap (S04) is the original analysis pipeline; wanglab-broad/starmap-py (S06) and wanglab-broad/starfinder (S07) are the modern Wang lab successors. A future Research Agent should re-verify maintenance state of each repository.

8. **STARmap benchmark grades**: The Wang 2018 paper's 160-gene and 1020-gene panels are the primary candidate benchmark datasets. Their Grade A/B/C/D assessment in `benchmarks.md` is constrained by (a) the supplementary tables (S02) not being directly retrieved, and (b) the absence of a public GEO/SRA accession. The ClusterMap raw images (S08) are an incomplete resource (no full codebook). Provisional grade: C (architecture-level) for V01 until the supplementary tables are inspected in a follow-up.
