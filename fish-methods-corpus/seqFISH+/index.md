# seqFISH+

## Official Name

seqFISH+; RNA seqFISH+ specifies the original RNA implementation. [S01, title and Fig. 1; reported; V01]

## Full Name

The authors describe seqFISH+ as an evolution of sequential fluorescence in situ hybridization. No separate expansion of the plus sign is defined. [S01, abstract; reported; V01]

## Aliases

RNA seqFISH+. `seqFISH-PLUS` is the official repository name, not a replacement method name. [S01, title; S06, README; reported; V01]

## Method Category

Multiplexed RNA FISH; sequential hybridization imaging; spatial transcriptomics. [S01, Fig. 1 and Methods, encoding strategy; reported; V01]

## Primary Application

Spatial and subcellular measurement of mRNAs from 10,000 genes in NIH/3T3 cells and mouse brain sections. [S01, Figs. 1–3; reported; V01]

## Original Publication

Eng, C.-H. L. et al. *Transcriptome-scale super-resolved imaging in tissues by RNA seqFISH+*. Nature **568**, 235–239 (2019). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-019-1049-y); PMID 30911168. [S01]

## Brief Summary

Primary DNA probes bind RNA and present readout-binding overhangs. Sequential fluorescent readouts sparsely label transcripts as pseudocolours; four barcoding rounds recover gene identities within each fluorescence channel. [S01, Fig. 1 and Methods; reported; V01]

## Known Versions

V01: original RNA seqFISH+ (2019), including separate cell and tissue preparation branches. Later DNA seqFISH+ is a related developer extension with different targets and probe chemistry; its resources must not be substituted for V01 RNA probes. [S01; S09; reported; see description.md]
