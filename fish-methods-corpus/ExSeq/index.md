# ExSeq

## Official Name

ExSeq [S01, title/abstract; reported].

## Full Name

Expansion Sequencing [S01, title/abstract; reported].

## Aliases

Expansion Sequencing; untargeted ExSeq; targeted ExSeq (author-used full name and variant labels) [S01, Figs. 1 and 4; reported].

## Method Category

Expansion microscopy combined with in situ RNA sequencing; targeted padlock/RCA and untargeted cDNA/RCA workflows [S01, Materials and methods; reported].

## Primary Application

Spatial RNA mapping, including subcellular localization and cell-type mapping in intact specimens [S01, Figs. 1-6; reported].

## Original Publication

Alon S. et al. (2021). Expansion sequencing: Spatially precise in situ transcriptomics in intact biological systems. *Science* 371(6528):eaax2656. [DOI: 10.1126/science.aax2656](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax2656). Published 2021-01-29 [S01, publication header; reported].

## Brief Summary

ExSeq anchors RNA to an expandable hydrogel, expands and re-embeds the specimen, and passivates gel carboxylates to permit enzymatic reactions. Untargeted ExSeq sequences amplified cDNA; targeted ExSeq sequences barcodes in amplified padlock probes ligated directly on RNA [S01, Materials and methods; reported].

## Known Versions

Internal corpus IDs distinguish meaningful architectures and encoding scopes; these are not author-numbered releases:

- V01: original untargeted ExSeq, random-primed RT/cDNA circularization and SOLiD in situ sequencing [S02, supplementary pp. 5-8].

- V03: original targeted breast cancer experiment, seven-symbol distance-3 barcodes and SOLiD readout [S02, pp. 29-31].
