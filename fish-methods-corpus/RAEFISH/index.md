# RAEFISH

## Official Name

RAEFISH. Reported: S01, Summary and Figure 1.

## Full Name

Reverse-padlock Amplicon Encoding Fluorescence In Situ Hybridization (S01, Summary). The main text also uses “Reverse-padlock Amplicon Encoding FISH.”

## Aliases

RAEFISH. Perturb-RAEFISH is a distinct experimental variant, not an interchangeable name for endogenous-transcriptome RAEFISH (S01, Figure 7).

## Method Category

Image-based spatial transcriptomics; multiplexed RNA FISH with rolling circle amplification (reported: S01, Figure 1).

## Primary Application

Spatial detection of targeted endogenous transcripts in cultured cells and tissue sections; variant for direct gRNA readout in CRISPR screens (reported: S01, Summary).

## Original Publication

Cheng Y. et al. (2025). *Sequencing-free whole-genome spatial transcriptomics at single-molecule resolution*. Cell. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2025.09.006). S01. An earlier author preprint is S06.

## Brief Summary

Adjacent reverse-padlock and priming probes recognize RNA. Splint-assisted circularization and rolling circle amplification produce targets for encoding probes, which are decoded by sequential fluorescence imaging (reported: S01, Figure 1 and STAR Methods).

## Known Versions

V01: endogenous-transcriptome RAEFISH, human and mouse libraries. V02: Perturb-RAEFISH gRNA variant, described in the same publication. These are corpus identifiers, not author release numbers. Preprint S06 is a publication-stage record, not a separate chemistry version.
