# TDDN-FISH

## Official Name

TDDN-FISH. Reported: S01, Title and Abstract.

## Full Name

Tetrahedral DNA Dendritic Nanostructure-Enhanced Fluorescence In Situ Hybridization (reported: S01, Abstract). The full name "Tetrahedral DNA Dendritic Nanostructure-Enhanced Multiplexed FISH" also appears (S01, Introduction).

## Aliases

TDDN-FISH. No other official aliases identified in the examined sources.

## Method Category

Image-based spatial transcriptomics; multiplexed FISH with DNA dendritic nanostructure signal amplification (reported: S01, Abstract and Introduction).

## Primary Application

Spatial detection and imaging of RNA targets in cultured cells and tissue sections, including short RNAs such as miRNAs, with single-cell and subcellular resolution (reported: S01, Abstract and Results).

## Original Publication

Wang YF., Chen HJ., He ZD., Wang ZG., Pang DW., Liu SL. (2025). *Tetrahedral DNA dendritic nanostructure-enhanced FISH for high-speed, sensitive spatial transcriptomics*. Nature Communications. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64294-1). S01.

## Brief Summary

TDDN-FISH employs hierarchical tetrahedral DNA dendritic nanostructures (TDDNs) for signal amplification in RNA FISH. A bifunctional primary probe (target-binding + readout sequence) hybridizes to RNA, then sequentially assembled T0, T1, and T2 tetrahedral DNA layers bind to the readout sequence, followed by fluorophore-labeled detection oligos. The method is enzyme-free, achieves ~8-fold faster single-round detection than HCR-FISH, generates stronger signals than smFISH, and supports short RNA targets including miRNAs. Iterative rounds with TCEP cleavage enable multiplexed color-coded detection (F^N encoding; F fluorescence channels × N imaging rounds) (reported: S01, Abstract and STAR Methods).
